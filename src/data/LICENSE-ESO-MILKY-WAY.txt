Adaptation in this application: downsampling, rank filtering of isolated point
sources, luminance conversion, slight smoothing, exclusion of off-plane objects,
star-based registration, reprojection into the observer's sky and illustrative
brightness adjustment. The 3.80-degree orientation correction was fitted to nine
HYG stars. Eight other reference stars were checked independently; the largest
measured alignment residual is 0.300 degree, suitable for the diffuse texture.
The cloud/dust morphology comes from the photograph. This is not a calibrated
surface-brightness map. Catalog stars and 2026 solar-system ephemerides are drawn
separately; the photographic point sources are suppressed, not used as objects.
