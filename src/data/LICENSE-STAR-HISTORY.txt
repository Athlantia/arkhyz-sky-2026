Star-name histories

Short original Russian and English retellings of verified entries in All Skies Encyclopaedia, https://ase.exopla.net/, by the IAU-WGSN Etymology Group, Susanne M Hoffmann, Youla Azkarrula, Ian Ridpath and contributing article authors. Specific source pages are linked from each star card. Source material: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Text has been condensed and translated; uncertain etymologies remain explicitly uncertain. Retrieved 8 September 2026.

Navi: NASA Apollo Lunar Surface Journal (Apollo 11 Return to Orbit), https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a11/a11.launch.html. Current Gamma Cassiopeiae name Tiansi verified in IAU-WGSN Catalogue of Star Names, https://exopla.net/star-names/modern-iau-star-names/ (adopted 13 November 2025).

These histories are editorial content, not astronomical position data. Stars without a curated history retain their measured catalog facts without generic filler.
